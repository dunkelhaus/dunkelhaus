# Experience logo sources

Assets are served locally from public/companies. Company names remain visible; logos are decorative with empty alt text to avoid duplicate announcements. Images reserve their dimensions and load lazily.

- Comcast: peacock mark extracted without redrawing from Comcast's own [open-source website SVG](https://github.com/Comcast/comcast.github.io/blob/main/docs/logo.svg). Original colors and geometry are retained; the wordmark is omitted because the employer name is rendered as text. Apache-2.0 and NOTICE files are included under public/licenses.
- Wells Fargo: wordmark from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/wellsfargo.svg), placed on a red tile. Simple Icons distributes its artwork under CC0.
- Persistent Systems: [NSE_PERSISTENT.svg](https://github.com/shareperks/indian-listed-company-logos/blob/main/nse/NSE_PERSISTENT.svg), a listed-company logo asset attributed to TradingView in the SVG source.

- Seceon: supplied by the user as a photograph of the three-leaf mark. The paper background and pale veins were removed to create a transparent asset, encoded losslessly as WebP; the provided emblem is the reference. A CSS brightness adjustment improves visibility on the dark page without changing the stored green asset.

These assets identify former/current employers; brand rights remain with their owners.
